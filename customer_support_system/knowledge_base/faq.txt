FAQ DOCUMENT — ABC Technologies

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered email address and you will receive a password reset link within 5 minutes.

Q: How do I upgrade my subscription plan?
A: Log in to your account, go to Settings > Subscription, and click "Upgrade Plan". Changes take effect immediately and you will be billed the prorated difference.

Q: Can I add more users to my plan?
A: Yes. For the Starter plan, additional users are $5/user/month. Professional and Enterprise plans include expanded user limits. Go to Settings > Team > Invite Users.

Q: How do I request an invoice?
A: Invoices are automatically generated each billing cycle and available under Billing > Invoices. You can download them as PDF.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refund requests are reviewed by our billing team and take 5–7 business days after approval to reflect on your original payment method.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings > Subscription > Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the billing period. Note: cancellations require supervisor approval and confirmation.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes, all plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Q: What file types can I upload?
A: Supported formats are PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG, and JPG. Maximum file size is 50 MB.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: Starter plan: email support. Professional: email + chat. Enterprise: 24/7 dedicated support.

Q: Where can I find my API key?
A: Settings > Integrations > API Keys. You can generate or revoke API keys from there.
