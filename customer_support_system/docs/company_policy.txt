COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT - ABC Technologies

1. REFUND POLICY
   - Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.
   - Annual subscriptions are eligible for a pro-rated refund within 60 days.
   - Refund requests must be submitted via the support portal.
   - All refund requests above $100 require supervisor approval.
   - Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

2. SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY
   - Customers may cancel their subscription at any time from the account dashboard.
   - Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
   - Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.
   - Cancellation requests for enterprise accounts must be approved by management.

3. ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY
   - Account closure is permanent and cannot be undone.
   - All data associated with the account will be deleted within 30 days.
   - Account closure requires identity verification.
   - Accounts with outstanding balances cannot be closed until payment is settled.

4. COMPENSATION POLICY
   - Compensation may be offered for service outages exceeding 4 hours.
   - Compensation typically includes service credits or subscription extensions.
   - All compensation requests must be reviewed and approved by a supervisor.

5. DATA PRIVACY POLICY
   - Customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
   - ABC Technologies does not sell or share customer data with third parties.
   - Customers may request a data export at any time.

6. ESCALATION POLICY
   - Tier 1: Standard support agents handle general queries.
   - Tier 2: Technical specialists handle complex technical issues.
   - Tier 3: Management escalation for unresolved or high-impact issues.
   - All escalations to management require supervisor sign-off.

7. SLA POLICY
   - Standard response time: 24 hours.
   - Priority support response time: 4 hours.
   - Enterprise support response time: 1 hour.
