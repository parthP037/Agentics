COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT — ABC Technologies

REFUND POLICY:
- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual subscriptions.
- Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable after the billing date.
- Refund requests must be reviewed and approved by a human supervisor.
- Processing time: 5–7 business days after approval.
- Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

CANCELLATION POLICY:
- Customers may cancel their subscription at any time.
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
- Subscription cancellation requests require human supervisor approval.
- Data is retained for 30 days after cancellation before deletion.

ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY:
- Account closure is permanent and irreversible.
- All customer data will be deleted after 30 days.
- Account closure requires human supervisor approval.
- Outstanding invoices must be settled before closure.

COMPENSATION POLICY:
- Compensation may be offered for service outages exceeding 2 hours.
- Compensation is subject to human supervisor review and approval.
- Forms: account credits, plan upgrades, or extended trial periods.

ESCALATION POLICY:
- Customers may request escalation to management at any time.
- Escalations are reviewed by a human supervisor before routing.
- Response time for escalated issues: within 24 hours.

DATA & PRIVACY:
- Customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit (AES-256).
- Data is never sold to third parties.
- GDPR and CCPA compliant.
